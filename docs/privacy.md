# Privacy policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

This policy covers the notesd apps for Android and iPhone, the notesd server, and this website.
notesd is made by Cassio Zen, one person working on it in their free time.

## The short version

I don't collect, store or see any of your data. You don't have an account with me. The apps have
no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no crash reporting.

## How notesd works

notesd is self-hosted. The apps only connect to the notesd server whose address you enter. That
server is run by you, or by someone you trust, such as a family member or friend. Your notes,
pictures and account are stored on that server, and whoever runs it is responsible for them. I
don't run these servers and have no access to them.

## On your device

So that they work offline, the apps keep a copy of your notes and pictures in their own private
storage, along with the server address and your sign-in details.

- On Android, this data is left out of cloud backups and device transfers.
- On iPhone, this data can be included in your device or iCloud backups, like any other app's
  data. Your backup settings decide this.
- Signing out keeps your notes on the device so that you can sign in again later. To delete
  everything, use **Settings → Remove all data**, or uninstall the app.

Locked notes are encrypted on your device before they're sent to the server. The server stores
only the encrypted version.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network access**, to connect to your server.
- **Local network** (iPhone), to reach your server when it's on your home network.
- **Face ID or fingerprint**, if you turn it on, to open locked notes. Your operating system
  handles this, and your biometric data never reaches the app or the server.
- **Photos** (iPhone), only for the pictures you choose to add to a note. The app can't see the
  rest of your library.

## Other services

notesd only uses other services when you turn on a feature that needs them. In both cases it's your
server that connects to the service, not me:

- **Emailing notes** sends the note through [Resend](https://resend.com), using an API key you
  provide. The [Resend privacy policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy) applies.
- **Checking for updates** asks GitHub whether there's a newer release. The
  [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
  applies.

Apple and Google collect some data when you download and use apps from their stores, such as
download counts and crash reports you agree to share. Their own privacy policies apply to that
data.

## This website

The website has no analytics and sets no cookies. The documentation pages load fonts from Google
Fonts. The web host may keep standard access logs.

## Children

notesd isn't made for children, and since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children
either.
