# Sharing with others

Your notesd server can hold a few accounts, for family or friends. Each account's notes are
private. To write notes together, share a space: everyone in it can read and edit all its notes.

## Add someone to the server

Admins do this in **Settings → Server → People**, in the web, Mac or Android app:

1. Choose **Invite someone…** and enter a username, and the name they'll be shown by if you like.
2. Send them the link. It works once, for 7 days.
3. They open it and choose a password.

Make the invitation with notesd open at the address they'll use, not `localhost`, or the link
won't work for them. More in [Accounts](../self-hosting/users.md#adding-people).

## Share a space

1. Choose the space's name at the top of the sidebar, then **Info**.
2. Under **People**, choose who to invite.
3. They choose **Join** in their space switcher.

notesd sends no emails or notifications, so let them know.

## Owners and members

You own the spaces you share. Only you can invite or remove people, rename the space or delete
it. Members can read and edit every note, and leave at any time.

To leave your own space, first make someone else the owner, or choose **Stop sharing**. The
space and its notes stay with you.

## Good to know

- People show by the name and photo they chose in **Settings → Server → Name and photo**, or else
  by their username.
- Locked notes can't be in a shared space. Your first space, "Notes", is never shared.
- Moving a note into or out of a shared space changes who can see it, so the app asks first.
- Leaving a space, or being removed, takes its notes off your devices. Edits that haven't synced
  yet are kept as copies in your own notes.
- The iPhone app can use shared spaces, but can't add people.

For who can see what, see the [security model](../../concepts/security-model.md#shared-spaces).
