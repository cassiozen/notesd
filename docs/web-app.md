# Installing the web app

Installed, notesd opens in its own window from your dock or home screen,
and keeps your notes on the device so it works without a connection. To
stop keeping them there, go to Settings → Sync & data.

It needs notesd served over HTTPS (or from `localhost`): on a plain-HTTP
address like `http://192.168.1.20:8080`, Chrome won't show its install
offer, and no browser keeps notes on the device. See
[Access from anywhere with HTTPS](hosting.md#access-from-anywhere-with-https).

## Chrome

- **Desktop:** in notesd, go to Settings → Sync & data and click
  **Install notesd**. Or click the install icon at the right of the
  address bar.
- **Android:** open the ⋮ menu, tap **Add to home screen**, then
  **Install**.

## Safari

- **Mac** (macOS 14 or later): choose File → **Add to Dock**.
- **iPhone and iPad:** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

On iPhone and iPad, the app on the home screen doesn't share sign-ins with
Safari, so you sign in once more the first time you open it.
